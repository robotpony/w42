## Opening hook

The plain announcement: the family cookbook is live at [bruce-loves-to-cook.ca](https://bruce-loves-to-cook.ca/), on its own domain, at build 4. Or open with the reader quote ("It makes me want to click things, which is the opposite of most recipe sites I visit.") and then name the site.

## Sections

1. **What it is.** Our family's recipes, moved out of a shared Google Doc and onto the web. Edited in Obsidian, built with Hugo. One line on why: the doc was never going to be smart, and it never read as a website to the kids.
2. **What's in it.** The counts, refreshed from `public/recipes/index.json` before publishing (post 1 has 223 recipes, 17 essays, 28 method pages, 14 reference pages, 39 food log pages). One sentence each on the sections: recipes, essays, methods, reference, and the Food Log.
3. **Three things to try.** A short tour, each with a link and maybe a screenshot from the not-a-chef agent:
   - A recipe with a formula diagram (the ratio drawn as a row of icons).
   - The burgers blueprint: parts, options and named builds on a timeline.
   - Win the fridge, the planning idea behind a lot of the weeknight cooking.
   [AUTHOR CHECK: swap in your own three favourites]
4. **What it isn't.** No ads, no life story before the ingredients, no popups. Recipes in an instructional voice, metric with imperial in brackets.
5. **What's coming here.** The series that follows: ten lessons (Oct 20), working with agents (Oct 27), smart content on plain markdown (Nov 3), drawing with text (Nov 10), and should you build one (Nov 17). Link each once they're live, or list titles only.
