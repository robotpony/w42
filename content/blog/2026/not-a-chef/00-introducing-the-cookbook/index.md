## Opening hook

The plain announcement: our family cookbook, [Bruce loves to cook](https://bruce-loves-to-cook.ca/), is live on its own domain, at build 4. It's a Hugo site, edited in Obsidian, with 223 recipes, 17 essays, 28 method pages, 14 reference pages and 39 engineering-style food log pages.
